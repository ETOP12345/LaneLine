Ranking method
  • Rank each event by personal-best time within the selected age group, division and course. Equal times share a rank.
  • Add each swimmer's six lowest event ranks. Lowest total wins; fewer than six events is incomplete and follows complete swimmers.
  • Ties: more distinct LCM AAA-or-faster events, then more SCY AAA-or-faster events, then higher actual top-six USA Swimming power-point total when available for everyone tied. Otherwise the rank stays tied; alphabetical order is display-only.
  • Scoring events follow the Zones event list; SCY uses 500 Free instead of 400 Free and also includes 100 IM. No Zones qualifying-date window is applied.
  • Top 12 and Top 14 indicate board positions, not team selection. No estimated power points are used.
